Welding shop follow-up text templates

Quick answer: The Welding Shop Operating Pack includes 5 follow-up texts: a post-quote nudge, a day-3 close, a job-done review ask, a 6-month repeat and a slow-payer reminder. This page shows two sample texts, a post-quote nudge and a job-done review ask, so you can see the style before you buy. The pack is $49 once, or $99 with a hosted booking page.

Examples only. Every bracketed value is a placeholder. Swap in your shop name, the customer's name and the job, and change the wording to sound like you.

1. After the quote: post-quote nudge

Send it a day or so after the quote goes out, when the customer has had time to look but has not replied.

Hi [Customer], it's [Your name] at [Shop]. Just checking that you got the quote for the [job]. Happy to answer questions or adjust anything. If it looks good, reply YES and I'll get you on the schedule.

2. After the job: review ask

Send it once the job is done and paid, while the work is still fresh for the customer.

Hi [Customer], thanks for having us out for the [job]. Hope it's holding up well. If you have a minute, a quick review helps a small shop like ours a lot: [review link]. If anything isn't right, text me here and I'll come fix it.

Why these are short

A text gets read on a phone between other things. Each one names the shop, names the job, and asks for one thing: a YES to book, or a review. The second text also gives the customer an easy way to tell you about a problem before they post about it.
